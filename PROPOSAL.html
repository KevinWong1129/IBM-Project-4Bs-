Executive Summary
TL;DR
IBM client-facing teams spend hours manually searching for and assembling product content before every client engagement. This project builds an intelligent RAG knowledge base that automatically ingests content from Box and Seismic, enables semantic search across all of it, and uses IBM Bob to generate polished client assets — one-pagers, deck outlines, product summaries — in seconds.
The Problem
"Every week I spend 2–3 hours hunting across Box and Seismic for the right case study, product sheet, and competitive talking points — then manually stitching them into a deck. By the time I'm done, I've lost half my prep time and the content might already be outdated."
— A typical IBM sales consultant, every single week
Core Pain Points
  • Content is scattered across Box folders, Seismic channels, and email threads
  • Assembling a one-pager or deck still requires 2–4 hours of manual work
  • No centralised, searchable source of truth for current IBM product content
  • Wrong or stale versions of documents get shared with clients
  • Institutional knowledge is locked in individuals — and leaves when they do
Proposed Solution
What We Are Building
A RAG-powered knowledge base that automatically ingests IBM product content from Box and Seismic, keeps it semantically indexed, and uses IBM Bob to generate accurate, source-grounded client assets on demand — all surfaced through a simple query interface.

Anticipated Challenges
  • 1
    API Access & Permissions
    Box and Seismic both require OAuth credentials and enterprise admin approval. As an intern, provisioning access can take days or weeks. Submit access requests on Day 1 and follow up proactively.
  • 2
    Document Format Diversity
    Ingested content will include PDFs, PPTX, DOCX, and HTML. Tables, embedded images, and slide layouts often break naive parsers. Budget time for format-specific handling.
  • 3
    Chunking Strategy
    How documents are split (by page, paragraph, or semantic boundary) directly affects retrieval quality. Too large = noisy; too small = lost context. Plan to experiment and evaluate multiple strategies.
  • 4
    Retrieval Quality & Hallucination
    Even with good chunking, the LLM can generate plausible-sounding but incorrect content if retrieved chunks are off-topic. Build an evaluation harness early to catch this systematically.
  • 5
    IBM Bob Integration Specifics
    Bob's API behaviour, rate limits, and prompt formats may differ from standard OpenAI-style interfaces. Budget time to understand its constraints before building prompts around assumptions.
  • 6
    Data Security & Governance
    Product content may be commercially sensitive. The vector database, generated outputs, and UI must be access-controlled. Follow IBM security policy: no credentials in code, no sensitive data in logs.
  • 7
    Evaluation is Subjective
    Measuring whether a generated one-pager is "good" is not straightforward. Define concrete evaluation criteria early — factual accuracy, brand tone, completeness — so iteration has clear direction.
  • 8
    Scope Creep
    It is tempting to keep adding features. Resist it. Get the ingestion pipeline and retrieval quality solid first. A simple working demo beats a complex half-finished one every time.
