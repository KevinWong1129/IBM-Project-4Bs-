01 — Overview

What is IBM Concert?

"A unified IT operations platform that connects signals, context, and teams across an enterprise's existing technology stack."

IBM Concert acts as the single pane of glass and coordination layer across applications, infrastructure, networks, and operations workflows — helping organizations cut through alert noise, understand what matters most, and respond more effectively.

Agentic AI Cross-domain No rip-and-replace

IBM Concert works with, not instead of, a customer's current tools. It ingests signals from across the environment, unifies them into shared context, and uses AI-powered agents to surface priorities, recommend actions, and support coordinated response across teams.


The result is faster issue resolution, improved resilience, and simpler operations — without displacing your existing investments.
